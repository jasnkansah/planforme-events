Meet Jasmine

Founder & Lead Planner

I'm Jasmine, Founder and Lead Planner of Plan For Me Events. As a Certified Wedding Planner with over 10 years of experience planning weddings, corporate events, and special celebrations, I bring both creativity and organization to every event I help coordinate.

After planning countless beautiful events, it is truly an honor to be trusted with life's most meaningful moments. I believe every event — whether a wedding, corporate gathering, or special celebration — should feel intentional, well-organized, and memorable.

My goal is to help clients navigate the planning process with clarity, confidence, and peace of mind — creating experiences that feel authentic and thoughtfully executed.
