How We Work Together

Built Around the Way You Plan

You're leading your own planning. These services are designed to meet you exactly where you are — whether you need expert direction for an hour, or someone to take over and run the day you've spent months building.

01 · Guidance Session

You Plan,
I Guide

$150

30-Minute Session

"This session is designed for couples who are planning their wedding independently and need professional guidance to move forward with confidence."

What Happens During Your Session

  • Review of your current planning stage
  • Answers to your specific wedding planning questions
  • Guidance on vendor selection and booking order
  • Basic timeline direction
  • Budget guidance and prioritization
  • Ceremony and reception flow suggestions
  • Identification of your immediate next steps

What You'll Receive After

  • Your Custom Priority Checklist — curated for your specific wedding, not a generic template
  • The Next 30 / Next 90 Priority Map — clear breakdown of your next steps in order
  • Vendor Priority Guide — who to book now, who can wait, and who you may not need
  • Timeline Foundations Sheet — key considerations based on your venue and vision
  • Budget Awareness Notes — where your money should and shouldn't go

"You walk away feeling like you have a planner in your corner — without the full retainer. That's the whole point."

02 · Deep Dive Session

You Plan,
I Guide Intensive

$350

60-Minute Session

"For the bride who is already in the thick of planning and wants a professional set of eyes on what she's already done — before it's too late to change it."

What Happens During Your Session

  • Full review of your current wedding plans
  • Discussion of your booked vendors and venue
  • Detailed timeline evaluation
  • Identification of planning gaps and potential issues
  • Strategic vendor guidance and refinement
  • Logistics deep dive — setup, flow, vendor timing
  • Personalized recommendations to improve your overall plan

Everything from the Standard Session, Plus

  • Contract Logistics Review — planner's eyes on your vendor and venue contracts, flagging anything that could create a problem on your wedding day
  • Venue Layout Consult — guest flow, bar placement, ceremony-to-reception flip logistics, accessibility
  • Your "Protect Your Day" Flag List — every risk, gap, or conflict spotted in your planning with specific action items

"The Intensive is the session for the bride who wants to stop hoping everything works out — and start knowing it will."
