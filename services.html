You're leading your own planning. These services are designed to meet you exactly where you are — whether you need expert direction for an hour, or someone to take over and run the day you've spent months building.

01 · Guidance Session

You Plan,
I Guide

$150

30-Minute Session

"This session is designed for couples who are planning their wedding independently and need professional guidance to move forward with confidence."

What Happens During Your Session

  • Overall wedding vision, current stage of planning, and any questions about the planning process
  • Venue capacity guidance — seating per table, table count, tenting space, and reception flow considerations
  • Timeline guidance — reviewing your current timeline and providing direction on best practices and key considerations
  • Budget review and guidance
  • Vendor selection support and preferred vendor recommendations
  • Backup plan and contingency review

What You'll Receive After

  • Wedding Day Timeline Blueprint — a customizable framework for you to build your own timeline, with guidance notes from the session
  • Venue capacity and flow notes — seating, tables, tenting, and reception considerations
  • Preferred vendor recommendations list
  • Budget guidance summary
  • Backup plan document
  • Emergency kit document

Not Included in This Session

  • Ceremony and reception layout recommendations — available in the Intensive only
  • Design consultation — available as a separate add-on ($200)

"You walk away feeling like you have a planner in your corner — without the full retainer. That's the whole point."

03 · Month of Coordinator

You Plan,
I Manage Petite

$1,500

Month of Coordinator  ·  Up to 20 Guests

"Designed for elopements, micro weddings, and intimate celebrations that still require professional coordination. You've done the planning — now it's time to hand it off."

Pre-Wedding (Beginning 45 Days Out)

  • One (1) 60-minute client/planner Zoom meeting
  • Collection of all vendor contact information and contracts
  • Creation of a comprehensive wedding day timeline
  • Creation of vendor contact and information sheet
  • Distribution of final timeline to all vendors
  • Venue walk-through (if applicable)
  • Final detail review call or email with client
  • Confirmation calls and emails to all vendors
  • Point of contact for all vendors in the final weeks
  • Creation of wedding party and family itinerary
  • Preparation of day-of emergency kit checklist

Wedding Rehearsal

  • Coordination and direction of wedding rehearsal
  • Guidance for wedding party — positioning, order of procession, and cues
  • Distribution of day-of itinerary to wedding party and family

Ceremony (Up to 2 Hours)

  • Coordinate vendor arrivals and setup
  • Oversee ceremony setup and décor placement
  • Coordinate and cue wedding party processional
  • Manage ceremony timeline and flow
  • Coordinate with officiant and musicians/DJ for cues
  • Manage guest seating and ushering flow
  • Coordinate recessional and transition to reception

Cocktail Hour & Reception (Up to 6 Hours)

  • Oversee cocktail hour setup and vendor transitions
  • Coordinate grand entrance and reception program flow
  • Manage reception timeline — first dance, toasts, dinner, cake cutting, and send-off
  • Communicate timeline updates to DJ, catering, and all vendors
  • Handle any issues or last-minute changes on the day
  • Coordinate vendor breakdown at the end of the event

"Small wedding, professional execution. Every detail handled so you can be present for every moment."
