How We Work Together

Built Around the Bride Who Leads Her Own Planning

You're doing the work. These services are designed to meet you exactly where you are — with the right level of support at the right moment in your planning journey. Every session is one-on-one, focused entirely on your wedding, and followed by personalized tools built specifically for you.

01

You Plan, I Guide

$150 · 30-Minute Session

"For the bride who needs a certified planner in her corner — without the full retainer."

Who This Is For

Designed for couples planning their wedding independently who need professional guidance to move forward with confidence. If you're overwhelmed by where to start — or unsure if you're on the right track — this is your next step.

During Your Session
  • Review of your current planning stage
  • Answers to your specific wedding planning questions
  • Guidance on vendor selection and booking order
  • Basic timeline direction and budget guidance
  • Ceremony and reception flow suggestions
  • Identification of your immediate next steps
Your Personalized Planning Packet — Within 48–72 Hours
  • Custom Priority Checklist — Not a template. Curated for your specific wedding, guest count, and timeline.
  • Next 30 / Next 90 Priority Map — What to do in the next month and the next three months. No guessing.
  • Vendor Priority Guide — Who to book now, who can wait, who you may not need — and why.
  • Timeline Foundations Sheet — Key considerations based on your venue and vision.
  • Budget Awareness Notes — Where your money should and shouldn't go.

You Plan, I Guide
Intensive

$350 · 60-Minute Session

"For the bride who's already deep in planning and wants a professional set of eyes on everything she's built — before it's too late to change it."

Who This Is For

The Intensive is for the bride who has already booked vendors and wants reassurance her plan is on track, or needs help refining details and logistics. A full review — everything you've built, examined by a certified planner.

During Your Session
  • Full review of your current wedding plans
  • Discussion of your booked vendors and venue
  • Detailed timeline evaluation and gap identification
  • Strategic vendor guidance and refinement
  • Logistics deep dive — setup, flow, vendor timing
  • Personalized recommendations to strengthen your plan
Everything from the Standard Session, Plus
  • Contract Logistics Review — Planner's eyes on your vendor and venue contracts. We flag mismatched setup times, cancellation terms, and clauses that put the burden on you.
  • Venue Layout Consult — We review your floor plan and tell you what a planner sees: guest flow, bar placement, ceremony-to-reception logistics.
  • Your "Protect Your Day" Flag List — Every risk, gap, or conflict — in plain language with specific action items.
