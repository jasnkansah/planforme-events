Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about guidance sessions, coordination, policies, and what to expect when you work with Plan For Me Events.

Guidance Sessions

A guidance session is a focused, one-time, one-on-one consultation with a certified wedding planner — designed for couples leading their own planning who need expert direction, clarity, and a clear path forward. Following your session, you receive personalized planning tools built specifically for your wedding within 48–72 hours.
You Plan, I Guide ($150) — ideal if you're early or mid-planning, need help knowing where to start, or have questions about vendors, budget, or timeline and haven't booked most vendors yet.

You Plan, I Guide Intensive ($350) — ideal if you're already deep in planning, have booked vendors, and want a professional to review your contracts, evaluate your timeline, and identify any gaps before it's too late to change anything.
Guidance sessions do not include vendor sourcing, booking, or ongoing vendor communication on your behalf. They do not include day-of coordination or wedding day presence, unlimited access or ongoing support between sessions, or refunds — all sessions and packages are non-refundable once booked.
Within 48–72 hours you receive a personalized planning packet built around your specific wedding. This includes your Custom Priority Checklist, a Next 30 / Next 90 Priority Map, a Vendor Priority Guide, a Timeline Foundations Sheet, and Budget Awareness Notes. Nothing in this packet is a template — it is built for your wedding specifically.
Absolutely — and they are especially valuable for both. Elopements in Florida involve permit considerations, officiant coordination, venue logistics, and photography timing. A guidance session helps you navigate all of it with confidence. Proposals benefit from planning guidance around location, timing, vendor coordination, and the details that make the moment unforgettable.

Month-of Coordination

Month-of coordination begins 45 days before your wedding and includes vendor confirmations, timeline creation, rehearsal coordination, and full in-person wedding day management. A guidance session gives you expert knowledge to plan better. Coordination gives you a professional to execute the plan you built. Your session fee is credited toward coordination if you decide to upgrade.
You Plan, I Manage Petite ($1,500) — designed for elopements, micro weddings, and intimate celebrations with up to 20 guests. Full coordination and wedding day management tailored to smaller celebrations.

You Plan, I Manage ($2,000) — designed for mid-size to larger traditional weddings with multiple vendors, a full ceremony and reception, and a detailed day-of timeline. Full-day management and coverage.
Coordination kicks off with a planning meeting 4–6 weeks before your wedding. From there, we review all vendor details, begin vendor confirmations, build your wedding day timeline, and coordinate your rehearsal. On your wedding day, we are your single point of contact for every vendor and every detail.
Yes — rehearsal coordination is included in the You Plan, I Manage package. For the Petite package, light rehearsal guidance is included where applicable based on the size and structure of the event.

Booking & Policies

Guidance sessions are one-time — not recurring or ongoing. After booking you will receive a confirmation with next steps and session details. Your personalized planning tools are delivered within 48–72 hours of your session. All bookings are non-refundable — please confirm your availability before booking. If you need to reschedule, reach out early and we will do our best to accommodate.
Click the "Let's Plan Together" button anywhere on this site to submit a short inquiry. We'll review your wedding details and confirm availability within 48 hours. Once confirmed, you'll receive a booking link and contract to make it official.
Our primary service area is Central Florida including Orlando, Kissimmee, Winter Haven, Daytona Beach, Tampa, Poinciana, and Haines City. We have also coordinated destination weddings and styled shoots in Illinois, New York, and beyond. Reach out and we'll let you know what's possible for your location.
Yes. If you book a You Plan, I Guide session and later decide to upgrade to a coordination package, your session fee ($150 or $350) is credited toward your coordination investment. The earlier you book, the more time we have to work together effectively.
