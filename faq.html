Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about guidance sessions, coordination, policies, and what to expect.

A guidance session is a focused, one-time, one-on-one consultation with a certified wedding planner — designed for couples leading their own planning who need expert direction. Following your session, you receive personalized planning tools within 48–72 hours.

  • You Plan, I Guide ($150) — early or mid-planning, need timeline help, haven't booked most vendors yet
  • You Plan, I Guide Intensive ($350) — vendors are booked, want contract review and timeline evaluation
  • Vendor sourcing, booking, or ongoing vendor communication on your behalf
  • Day-of coordination or wedding day presence
  • Unlimited access or ongoing support between sessions
  • Refunds — all sessions and packages are non-refundable once booked

Month-of coordination begins 45 days before your wedding and includes vendor confirmations, timeline creation, rehearsal coordination, and full in-person wedding day management. Guidance sessions give you expert knowledge to plan better. Coordination gives you a professional to execute the plan you built. Your session fee is credited toward coordination if you upgrade.

  • You Plan, I Manage Petite ($1,500) — elopements, micro weddings, and intimate ceremonies
  • You Plan, I Manage ($2,000) — mid-size to larger weddings with full ceremony and reception
  • Guidance sessions are one-time — not recurring or ongoing
  • Personalized planning tools delivered within 48–72 hours after your session
  • All bookings are non-refundable — please confirm availability before booking
  • Need to reschedule? Reach out early and I will do my best to accommodate
