Latoya & DeVar | Orlando, FL

A Tropical Catholic Celebration at Paradise Cove

Jasmine NkansahCertified Wedding Planner · Plan For Me Events

Latoya and DeVar's wedding was a full sensory experience. Paradise Cove was decorated with palm leaves, birds of paradise, orchids, and peonies in vibrant tropical colors. The ceremony aisle was lined with lush greenery leading to a white arch adorned with tropical blooms, and the couple's Catholic ceremony was intimate and deeply heartfelt — until the Florida weather had other plans.

When unexpected rain arrived mid-ceremony, the Paradise Cove team moved everything to the pavilion without skipping a beat. The couple finished their vows surrounded by their loved ones, and when the sun came back out, so did the celebration.

"Jasmine and Plan For Me Events was God sent. My day would not have been as great without the detail-oriented and structured planning of Jasmine!"

The reception featured white polyester linens with coral napkins and vintage gold charger plates. Tall glass vase centerpieces overflowed with tropical blooms, and each table was named for a country Latoya and DeVar had visited together — with a photo of them taken there.

Personal touches were everywhere: personalized pens as favors, mini Jamaican black cakes honoring Latoya's culture, and a guest book box where loved ones wrote advice for the couple. The dance floor stayed packed all night, and a glow stick grand exit closed out one of the most joyful weddings I've had the privilege of coordinating.
