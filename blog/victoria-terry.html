Victoria & Terry | Lake Mary, FL

A Black Fairytale — Cinnamon, Gold & Pampas at Lake Mary Events Center

Jasmine NkansahCertified Wedding Planner · Plan For Me Events

Victoria and Terry came to me with a vision: "A Black Fairytale." In cinnamon, brown, rust, tan, gold, and black, they brought that vision fully to life at the Lake Mary Events Center.

The ceremony took place in the breathtaking rotunda room, flooded with natural light, with seating arranged in a semi-circle around the couple — a meaningful nod to their love for family. The ceremony concluded with a broom jump, a beautiful tribute to African American tradition.

"Jasmine far exceeded our expectations! As a wedding planner one must be prompt, organized, detail oriented, display excellent communication skills — and she far exceeded all of them."

Victoria wore a custom mermaid gown from a New York City boutique; Terry was impeccable in a custom ivory and cinnamon suit.

The reception floor plan was an X-shape with a mix of round and banquet tables, champagne and ivory satin linens, and centerpieces featuring pampas, natural palms, toffee roses, bleached Italian ruscus, and bunny tails. Floating candles and votives filled the room with warmth. The sweetheart table on the stage was framed by two Harlow stands, rose petals, and a gold chalice trio. The night ended with the couple dancing on a cloud before making their grand sparkler exit in a Tesla Model X.
