Janele & Malcolm | Glenville, IL

Ivory, Blush & Champagne at Abbington Distinctive Banquets

Jasmine NkansahCertified Wedding Planner · Plan For Me Events

Janele and Malcolm's wedding was magical from start to finish. The Abbington Distinctive Banquets in Glenville, Illinois provided an elegant backdrop for this ethereal, romantic celebration. Ivory and blush florals filled the ceremony and reception spaces, with the sweetheart table backdrop, candy table, and selfie booth all curated to perfection.

One of the most touching moments of the day came when Janele surprised Malcolm with a customized pen for his suit — a tribute to his late mother. The room felt the weight of that love.

"PlanForMe made both my proposal and my wedding night exceptional. They helped honor my mother who had to watch from above."
— Malcolm

Janele was stunning in a flowing A-line lace long-sleeve gown, and Malcolm and his groomsmen looked classic in light gray suits. The reception was a celebration in every sense: great food, great music, and a dance floor that never cleared.
