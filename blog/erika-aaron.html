Erika & Aaron | Winter Park, FL

A Jewish–Persian Fusion Celebration at Winter Club Event Venue

Jasmine NkansahCertified Wedding Planner · Plan For Me Events

If sunshine and smiles were a wedding, it would be Erika and Aaron's. The couple exchanged vows in the outdoor garden at the Winter Club Event Venue in Winter Park, Florida, with Aaron's childhood rabbi presiding over a ceremony that beautifully wove together Jewish and Persian traditions.

Erika honored her Persian heritage with a sofreh table filled with symbolic items — honey, spices, and coins representing the blessings of marriage. The couple's dogs Rudy and Molly walked down the aisle, adding a sweet personal touch that guests adored.

"All of my vendors and guests complimented her organization and communication. Even my father was impressed!"

Erika was radiant in a champagne A-line gown with a hooded veil, while her bridesmaids wore mustard-colored dresses that played beautifully against the garden florals. Aaron and his groomsmen were sharp in coordinating dark and light gray suits.

The indoor Italian rustic reception space was dressed with gray polyester linens, mason jars filled with lemons and limes, and warm votive candlelight. A gelato bar and custom cake closed out the night before the couple made their sparkler exit in the courtyard.
