DJ or Live Band: How to Decide for Your Wedding

Music sets the tone for your entire reception. Here's how to think through this decision.

Jasmine NkansahCertified Wedding Planner · Plan For Me Events

Music sets the tone for your entire reception — so this decision matters more than most couples realize. There's no universally right answer, but there is a right answer for your wedding.

The Case for a DJ

DJs offer versatility, affordability, and flexibility that's hard to beat. They can move seamlessly across genres, read the room and adjust the energy in real time, and accommodate song requests on the fly. For couples with diverse guest lists or specific must-play songs, a DJ is often the more practical choice.

DJ costs in Central Florida typically range from $1,200 to $2,500 depending on experience and hours. Most packages include a microphone for toasts and MC services throughout the reception.

The Case for a Live Band

A live band brings an energy and presence that recordings simply can't replicate. The performance itself becomes part of the entertainment, and for couples who want their reception to feel like an experience rather than a dance party, a band delivers that.

The trade-offs are real: cost (live bands in Central Florida typically start at $3,500–$5,000+), space requirements, and a more limited song catalog. Not every venue can accommodate a full band.

"If budget is a priority, go with a DJ. If ambiance and live entertainment are at the top of your list and your venue can accommodate it, a band is a memorable investment."

Questions to Ask Before You Book Either

  • Can I hear a sample or see a live performance before booking?
  • How do you handle song requests — and do you honor do-not-play lists?
  • Have you performed at my specific venue before?
  • What equipment do you bring, and what does the venue need to provide?
  • What's your contingency if something goes wrong (equipment failure, illness)?

Either way, vet your options carefully, ask for references from other weddings, and book as early as possible — the best vendors fill up fast.
