Necole & Nicholas | Daytona Beach, FL

Rust, Ivory & Champagne at the CiCi Hyatt Brown Museum

Jasmine NkansahCertified Wedding Planner · Plan For Me Events

Necole and Nicholas were originally set to marry in October, but Hurricane Ian had other plans. That setback only seemed to make their March wedding more meaningful. They exchanged vows on the stunning staircase inside the CiCi Hyatt Brown Museum in Daytona Beach — surrounded by an extraordinary art collection and the warmth of everyone they love.

The florals in rust, ivory, burnt orange, and tan felt perfectly suited to the museum's warmth. Rust and champagne linens carried the palette through the reception, where DJ Mike kept the energy up all night.

"Having Jasmine there during our big day made things flow so smoothly and stress free. She was always available and made sure we had everything we needed."

Necole was breathtaking in an ivory embellished mermaid gown with a rhinestone hairpiece. Nicholas was equally sharp in a custom rust and ivory suit. Every detail was considered — from the photo booth that had guests laughing all evening to the beautifully adorned cake that served as a showstopper centerpiece.

A day full of resilience, love, and joy.
