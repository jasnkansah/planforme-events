Which Wedding Planning Package Is Right for You?

Not every couple needs the same level of support. Here's how to figure out where you are.

Jasmine NkansahCertified Wedding Planner · Plan For Me Events

Not every couple needs the same level of support, and that's exactly why different planning packages exist. Here's a straightforward breakdown — and how to figure out which one fits you.

You Plan, I Guide — $150

This is the session for the newly engaged bride who doesn't know where to start, or the self-planning bride who has started but isn't sure she's doing it right. One focused hour with a certified planner, followed by a personalized planning packet built specifically for your wedding.

If you've been engaged for less than six months, have most of your vendors unbooked, and need clarity on what to do first — this is your session.

You Plan, I Guide Intensive — $350

This is the session for the bride who is already deep in planning. You've booked vendors, you've made decisions — and you want a professional to review what you've built before it's too late to change anything. Contract review, timeline evaluation, a full gap analysis.

If your wedding is within six months and you've already made most of your vendor decisions, this is your session.

You Plan, I Manage Petite — $1,500

Month-of coordination for elopements, micro weddings, and intimate celebrations up to 20 guests. You planned it — now someone professional executes it so you can be fully present.

You Plan, I Manage — $2,000

Full month-of coordination for traditional weddings. Everything in the Petite package plus a full day of management, a detailed wedding day timeline, rehearsal coordination, and complete vendor oversight from 4–6 weeks out.

"The right package depends on your time, your bandwidth, and how much of the process you want to own. When in doubt, reach out — we're happy to help you figure out where you need support."

Not Sure? Start Here.

If you're genuinely unsure which package you need, book a You Plan, I Guide session. After one hour together, we'll both know exactly where you are in the process and what kind of support will serve you best.
