Hire Your Wedding Planner Before Anyone Else

Why the first booking you make should be your planner — not your venue.

Jasmine NkansahCertified Wedding Planner · Plan For Me Events

Most couples start their planning by booking a venue — but the smarter move is to hire your wedding planner first. Here's why.

You Get Strategy Before You Sign Anything

A professional planner brings expertise, vendor connections, and budget strategy to the table from day one. Rather than booking vendors on your own and hoping they work well together, your planner helps you build a cohesive team based on your style, timeline, and budget. They've worked these rooms before. They know which vendors deliver and which ones leave couples scrambling.

You Avoid the Mistakes That Cost the Most

Beyond vendor management, a planner handles the details that couples often don't know to think about — contract clauses, setup windows, timeline logistics, and the hundred small things that separate a smooth wedding day from a stressful one.

"Hiring a planner early doesn't limit your planning — it makes everything else you do more intentional, more efficient, and more protected."

You Have Someone in Your Corner When Things Go Wrong

And something always does. A vendor cancels. A timeline falls apart. A contract has a clause nobody caught. When something unexpected happens, your planner is already there with a solution — because they've seen it before.

So What's the First Step?

Before you tour a single venue, book a You Plan, I Guide session. One focused hour with a certified wedding planner who will tell you exactly where to start, what to prioritize, and what to protect. The $150 investment comes off your coordination package if you decide to upgrade.
